# Eight Hours to Make Coffee

Full Sam / LitAI narrative and animation score. **Script draft for review; not yet
voiced, rendered or demonstrated.** The approved 38-second pilot establishes the
cast and animation treatment only. Research and claim limits are in
[RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md); realization requirements are in [PRODUCTION.md](PRODUCTION.md).

Audience: experienced engineers with working repositories, heterogeneous targets,
and a habit of outsourcing their first useful feedback to a distant pipeline.
Sam is technically capable, skeptical and increasingly inventive about defending
that habit. LitAI is female, quick, dry and willing to puncture his excuses. She
wins by showing useful work, not by making every objection foolish.

**Performance notation.** Each numbered turn specifies exact speech, a phrase cue,
speaker action, listener response and audience focus/hold. Times are not guessed:
production maps these phrases to the newly recorded dialogue. Where no quoted
cue is specified, use the opening spoken phrase for the authored action; an
opening hold may already be established before speech. A quiet listening
pose is intentional. One focal action at a time; no recurring arm wave, obligatory
nod, idle camera drift or synchronized reactions. Reserve a small pause for a
punchline, without canned laughter. Project names and code must remain readable.

**Visual spine.** A shared workbench, the existing-source folder, an “eight hours”
card, and Sam's accumulating excuse cards: COFFEE, LAUNDRY, GARAGE, THINKING.
These are stage props, not measured execution. LitAI progressively separates the
cards from the engineering graph. The graph, native targets and evidence become
the story's real activity. The closing coffee can happen beside productive work.

## 01 — The artisanal feedback loop

**Teaching job:** Establish the waste and Sam's emotional attachment to it.
**Evidence:** [F1, F2](RESEARCH.md#framework-authority). The eight-hour anecdote is
fictional dialogue, not a claim about any inspected repository's pipeline.

**01.01 · Sam:** “I have a proper engineering process. Commit, push, wait eight hours. Plenty of time to make coffee.”

*Play:* On “proper,” Sam squares a tidy PIPELINE card. On “eight hours,” he puts an
8 HOURS card beside it with misplaced pride. *Listen:* LitAI studies the card,
then looks at Sam. *Focus/hold:* Read the number; no animated fake stopwatch.

**01.02 · LitAI:** “Eight hours? Are you making coffee, Sam, or establishing diplomatic relations with the bean?”

*Play:* She looks once from the card to his small mug, then holds his eyeline.
*Listen:* Sam glances down at the mug. *Focus/hold:* Mug, then Sam; a short dry pause.

**01.03 · Sam:** “The pipeline is thorough. Builds. Tests. Platforms. It takes time. You can't rush quality.”

*Play:* He lays out three legitimate requirement cards rather than waving at the
screen. *Listen:* LitAI gives them her full attention. *Focus/hold:* Keep these
cards: the argument will preserve their rigor.

**01.04 · LitAI:** “Some work really takes hours. Discovering a missing tool after all that waiting is not thoroughness. It's a very expensive calendar reminder.”

*Play:* She marks a hypothetical MISSING TOOL note at the entrance to the graph.
*Listen:* Sam stops reaching for the mug. *Focus/hold:* Early prerequisite failure,
labelled “illustration”; no invented terminal result.

**01.05 · Sam:** “CI is where we find out whether the change works.”

*Play:* He pushes all three cards toward the distant CI end. *Listen:* LitAI follows
the movement without interrupting. *Focus/hold:* The empty space before CI.

**01.06 · LitAI:** “It should not be the first place your change meets a compiler. Let me show you what those eight hours have been concealing.”

*Play:* She opens a working area before CI and returns BUILD and TEST to it while
leaving an independent CI checkpoint in place. *Listen:* Sam leans his attention
toward the workbench. *Focus/hold:* “Earlier feedback. Required CI stays.”
