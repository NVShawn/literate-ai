## Reproducible research evaluation

`python3 scripts/summarize_research_trials.py TRIALS.json --baseline plain-cli`
validates a `literate-ai/research-trials@1` document with a `trials` array. Each row
names `task`, `trial`, `variant`, `model`, `environment`, `specification_identity`,
`oracle_identity`, boolean `passed`, and nonnegative integer `elapsed_ms`, `tokens`,
`cost_microusd`, `human_interventions`. Costs must come from the retained run's
pricing provenance; this helper does not look up pricing or invent missing costs.
Use the same task/trial pairs and model/environment/authority in each comparison.
Keep raw evidence alongside the input and never include secrets or hidden oracles.

The summary reports actual totals and paired task-weighted differences, with a
deterministic task-level bootstrap interval. Repeated trials of one task do not
inflate the independent sample size. Fewer than 20 tasks are flagged as a small
sample; a single task has no interval. These summaries describe supplied data,
not authenticated acceptance or demonstrated product superiority. The unit-test
fixture is synthetic and supplies no evidence of efficacy. Live experiments remain
unmeasured until their retained records exist.
