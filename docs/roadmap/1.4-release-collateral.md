# 1.4 release documentation and collateral refresh

- **Status:** active
- **Owning queue item:** [RELEASE-COLLATERAL-001](active-work.md#release-collateral-001-refresh-all-user-facing-documentation-and-collateral-for-each-release)
- **Completion / archival evidence:** pending until the final 1.4 candidate and its published assets have been reviewed

The 1.4 release must ship with current user-facing material drawn from the same
qualified source revision as the software. A release gate remains open when a
current page, film, deck, caption or download claim still describes 1.3.1 or the
AI Unprompted conference as the current product introduction. Keep genuinely
historical 1.3.1 and conference records labeled as such; do not rename their
artifacts or change their evidence to imply 1.4 qualification.
