- **Completion / archival evidence:** Read-only classification on 2026-10-05 of the

## Scope

The branch adds 54 test modules under `tests/critical`. Four were reconciled while
restoring the ten modules the audit had deleted or trimmed:
`test_accept_reservation_admission`, `test_accept_runtime_custody`,
`test_finalize_execution_policy` and `test_retained_source_cache_trust`. The other 50
hold 291 test methods.

| Verdict | Tests | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Keep (critical) | 56 | Names a security, custody, tamper, credential, sandbox or data-loss invariant. Usually becomes the subTest table that absorbs merges. |
| Move | 14 | 8 to `tests/e2e`, 6 to `tests/smoke`; they drive real receivers, children or Git. |
| Merge | 130 | Repeats another case per phase, option or controller. |
| Drop | 83 | Wire or schema round trip, mock replica, argument or type validation, or internal scheduling shape. |
| Unsure | 8 | The policy does not decide; see the per-module notes. |

The result would be about 70 tests in place of 291. Overlap between the kept cases
and existing E2E coverage was not checked.

## Repeated patterns

The main finding is per-phase repetition. The audit trimmed BUILD to two critical
tests per module (`test_action_build_action`, `test_action_build_result`,
`test_command_builder`) plus four in `e2e/test_action_build_process`. The 1.2 modules
repeat the same checks for GENERATE, LINK, PACKAGE and FINALIZE.

- **F1, dispatch request checks** (wrong worker, phase, predecessor or deadline;
  missing, extra or corrupt records): `action_finalize`, `action_generate_action`,
  `action_link`, `action_package`, `action_package_worker` and
  `action_link_dependencies`. Keep one phase-by-change table, represented by
  `action_generate_action`.
- **F2, cancellation before storage access or launch:** seven copies.
  `e2e/test_action_build_process::expiry_and_cancellation_interrupt_a_running_child`
  already covers the shared runner.
- **F3, record format** (round trip, closed envelope, non-canonical bytes, wrong hash,
  expiry): about 25 tests across the seven `*_record`/`*_result` modules and
  `action_retained_source`. Drop as wire/schema round trips.
- **F4, rehashed record substitutes another lock, plan, package or input:** a real
  tamper invariant repeated per phase. Representatives:
  `action_package_record::substituted_package_authority_refuses_even_when_rehashed`
  for inputs and `action_generate_result::foreign_input_or_allocation_cannot_rebind…`
  for results.
- **F5, supervised child limits** (reserved environment controls, no launch on bad
  input, cancellation, deadline, non-zero exit, output overflow):
  `action_finalize_process`, `action_generate_process` and parts of
  `configured_generate_worker`. Fold into `e2e/test_action_build_process` as phase
  subTests.
- **F6, authority revoked mid-operation:** about 20 tests. Representatives:
  `action_finalize_grant::file_revocation_stops_running_supervised_child` and
  `action_generate_import::authority_loss_during_fetch…`.
- **F7, corrupt fetched or returned bytes, no remote fallback:** about 15 tests.
  Representatives: `action_generate_import::last_corrupt_blob_does_not_publish…` and
  `action_retained_source::corrupt_local_blob_does_not_fall_back`.
- **F8, cleanup must not delete a foreign replacement:** six copies. Representative:
  `action_generate_execution::replaced_stage_is_not_deleted…`.
- **F9, command-controller capacity reservation and recheck:** `command_acceptor`,
  `command_generator`, `command_linker`, `command_packager`, `command_finalizer` and
  `registered_generation`. `command_packager` and `command_finalizer` share eight
  test names. Slot accounting is not a security invariant; keep one smoke
  representative.
- **F10:** the final branch loop in `legacy_release_line` repeats
  `published_release_identity::rehashed_record…`.
- **F11/F12, real LINK and GENERATE happy paths:** `command_link_dependencies` and
  `command_generate_worker` contain the other four to seven copies.

## Most valuable tests

These drive real processes, receivers or Git and should survive any reduction:

- `command_link_dependencies::packaging_chain_reopens_all_provider_proofs_in_real_receiver`:
  real build/test/execute, LINK through a receiver subprocess in dependency order,
  PACKAGE and FINALIZE proof, then refusal of a corrupt provider proof. Move to
  `tests/e2e`.
- `command_generate_worker` (live pool, receiver subprocess, HTTP CAS and child): move
  to `tests/e2e` next to `test_command_build_worker`.
- `action_finalize_grant::file_revocation_stops_running_supervised_child`.
- `local_accept_handoff`: real local commands plus an isolated ACCEPT child; move to
  `e2e/test_configured_accept_worker`.
- `legacy_release_line`, `published_release_identity` and
  `release_semver_requirement` (real Git with a bare remote): move to
  `e2e/test_project_releases`.

## Other findings

- **Fixtures rebuilt per test:** support fixtures are TestCase classes chained up to
  six deep (`FinalizeGrant` → `FinalizeAuthority` → `FinalizeWorkerInput` →
  `PackageWorkerInput` → `StandardArtifactAssembly` → `StandardProjectLifecycle`),
  and each `setUp` rebuilds the chain. The policy asks for expensive fixtures to be
  built once per class.
- **Duplicated setup:** each `tests/support/fixtures_test_action_*.py` duplicates the
  `setUp` of its critical module.
- **Private-state coupling:** many tests reach into internals such as
  `registry._paths`, `linker._completed`, `pool._facts` and
  `ports._execution_evidence`.
- **`test_explicit_action_inputs`** claims a real command-process transport, but its
  module-level `_WORKER` script is never used; the test runs nothing.
- **Wrong docstrings:** `test_later_phase_admission` ("Shared test fixtures extracted
  from test_action_admission") and `test_source_transfer_revocation`.
